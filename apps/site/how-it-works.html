How it works

One message in. Five honest offers out.

MoranBuy is a reverse marketplace: instead of you searching shops, shops' products come to you, ranked by what you can afford first and how close they are second. Here is everything that happens in between.

1 · Understand

Your words become a precise request.

"Nina laki tatu na nusu ya sofa ya watu watatu, nipo Sinza, delivery." Muse Spark reads Swahili, English and the mix people really speak, and returns a structured request that the software checks before using it.

  • Product, must-have features, budget, place, delivery or pickup, and when you plan to buy.
  • Swahili amounts are understood exactly: "laki tatu na nusu" is TSh 350,000.
  • Voice notes are decoded on the server and heard by Muse Spark 1.2 audio; photos are read by Muse Spark vision.
  • If something important is missing, MoranBuy asks one short question instead of guessing.

The request MoranBuy builds from the message above. Without Muse, plain Swahili rules produce the same shape.

2 · Rank

Budget first, location second. Always by rules, never by a model.

Every offer gets a known total: price plus known fees plus known delivery. Unknown delivery is shown as "not confirmed", never counted as zero. Then the ranking is fixed code you can read in the evidence of every search.

3 · Pay, deliver, release

Payment protection, step by step.

Shop accepts

With the delivery fee, so the total is final.

You pay MoranBuy

Mobile money through Snippe; the money is held.

Receipt + code

Your private code is stored only as a one-way hash.

Check, then code

The rider sends a photo and your code.

Release or refund

48 hours later, or at once when you confirm.

  • Wrong product at the door: don't give the code. The rider records the reason and a photo, you confirm, you are refunded and the shop gets a strike.
  • "Not received" after the correct code was used is refused: only you had the code.
  • Three wrong codes lock the order for an administrator.
  • A shop that doesn't send a rider within 48 hours: you are refunded automatically.
  • Disputes keep the money held until a super admin decides, with the full evidence timeline.
  • Every receipt carries a QR code with the signed order summary that anyone can verify.

Questions

What people ask.

Is MoranBuy the seller?

No. The shop named on an offer sells the product and is responsible for it. MoranBuy finds the offers, holds your payment under protection and keeps the record.

Does an AI decide my order or my refund?

No. Meta's models understand messages, photos and voice notes, check delivery photos and summarise disputes. Ranking, prices, payments, refunds and dispute decisions are fixed rules and people.

What if the delivery fee isn't known?

The offer says "delivery not confirmed" and is never treated as free. The shop must confirm the fee before you are asked to pay.

Which languages work?

Swahili, English and the mix of both, by text or voice note. Replies can be spoken back in Swahili or English.

Do I need an app or an account?

No. Use WhatsApp, or the web chat. Shops use WhatsApp too, with an optional dashboard.
