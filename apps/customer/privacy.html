English

What we collect. Your WhatsApp number (or a browser token on the web), the WhatsApp name you share, the messages you send MoranBuy, product photos you send, the location you describe or share, your budget and needs, and the orders you place.

How we use it. To understand your request, find and rank offers, pass your order to the merchant you chose (the merchant receives your number, the product, the quantity and the place for delivery), and tell you the order's status. Your messages may be read by an AI model (Meta's Muse Spark) to understand the request; prices and ranking are decided by code, not by the AI.

What we share. Only with the merchant of your order and with technical providers (Meta WhatsApp Business Platform, Meta Model API). We do not sell your data. A merchant does not gain marketing permission because you ordered; marketing needs your separate consent.

Consent and opting out. Reply STOP to end marketing and follow-up from every merchant through MoranBuy. Consent history is kept as evidence.

Retention and your rights. Order records are kept for bookkeeping; conversations are kept as long as needed to provide the service. You may ask to see, correct or delete your data by contacting us; the merchant receives the deletion request for what it holds. Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Act (2022) applies.

Security. Data is stored on protected servers, phone numbers are masked in logs and reports, and administrator access is audited.

Contact. Moran AI, Dar es Salaam, Tanzania, via moranai.co.tz/contact.
