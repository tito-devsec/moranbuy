# Changelog

All notable changes to MoranBuy are recorded here. Dates are in UTC.

## Unreleased

### 2026-10-28
- Redis work queue: scheduled campaign starts, campaign dispatch, follow-up tasks and customer reminders run from Redis sorted sets within a second of being due (deduplicated job ids, leases, a reaper for jobs whose worker died, retries with growing delays, a dead list admins can send back). Postgres stays the record: a feeder loads what falls due in the next two minutes, every handler re-reads Postgres and handles only the item named, and the old passes run every five minutes as a safety net, or alone with `QUEUE_MODE=poll` or when Redis is down. Queue state shows in Integration Health.
- Muse cost reporting: admins enter the token rates (Meta's price is not in the API response), every request is priced when it is recorded, past unpriced requests can be priced, and a report shows cost by day, feature and business, month to date with a projection, in dollars and shillings. A monthly budget alerts at 80% or when the projection passes it; with the hard cap on, Muse stops at the budget and every duty uses its plain fallback until the budget is raised or the month turns.
- Admin Products and Locations pages across all businesses (Super Admin and the fallback admin). Products: search, filters, sorting, paging, the problems that hurt ranking per product and in total (not updated recently, no photo, stock unknown, out of stock, no type, business has no placed location, hidden), hide from customer search or show again with an audited reason (stored apart from the business's own switch, so neither the business nor a Moran sync undoes it; the business sees the reason), CSV export with spreadsheet formulas neutralised. Locations: filters, coverage by region, what each location is missing, businesses selling with no placed location, and admin pin placement with region and district filled from the address; Moran-owned locations are corrected in Moran AI.
- File uploads: businesses upload product photos (public at `/media/<random name>`) and verification documents (PDF or image, private to the business and admins, every admin view audited, attached to the verification request and safe from deletion while it is pending); web customers send a photo of what they want after accepting the terms, kept private and read by Muse as data. Types are read from the bytes (JPEG, PNG, WebP, PDF), sizes and a daily count are capped, names are random. Expired customer photos and product photos no listing uses are purged from disk with their records.
- Muse admin summary: "Explain with Muse" on Overview, Insights, Campaigns, Outreach, Follow-up, Orders, Billing, Muse Spark and Retention in Super Admin. Muse reads the section's counts (no phone numbers or message text) and explains what needs attention; rule checks are always shown beside it, and Muse's words are kept only when every number is in the data.
- Campaign scheduling: confirming takes an optional send time (at least five minutes ahead, within sixty days, 08:00 to 21:00 Tanzania time); the recipient ledger is written at confirmation, every gate is re-run when the time comes, and a gate that no longer holds fails the campaign with the reason instead of sending. Unschedule returns it to ready; pausing and resuming before the time keeps the schedule.
- Retention purge: each kind of record has a keep period (editable in Super Admin → MoranBuy → Retention, with floors), a worker purges in batches every six hours, `RETENTION_DRY_RUN` (default true) makes it count only, and a super admin can preview or purge by hand with a reason. Orders, payments, consent events, contacts, merchants, listings and the audit trail are never purged; old orders lose only the delivery location.
- Request fields now act: a requested variant (size, colour, capacity) sets the price and stock an offer is ranked on and is carried to selection, the order, the owner alert and the Moran handoff; "bila mbadala" shows exact matches only and says how many close offers were hidden ("mbadala sawa" shows them); urgency ("leo", "haraka", "kesho") puts offers in stock now first within the same price band, never above a cheaper band. The plain reader also reads storage sizes and colours, and "simu" or "kochi" match smartphone and sofa listings.
- Lead conversion: when the merchant accepts, is paid for or delivers an order, the customer's earlier follow-up leads with that merchant (open, or stopped by their reply, within 30 days) become converted with the order recorded; a rejected or cancelled order converts nothing.
- Schedule reminders: a worker sends the reminder a customer asked for when it falls due, outside quiet hours (21:00 to 08:00 East Africa Time), as text inside 24 hours of their last message or as the approved template after that, never after STOP or once they ordered, retried up to three times; a YES reply reruns their earlier search. Wording and template in Super Admin → MoranBuy → Notifications; `REMINDER_DRY_RUN` defaults to true.
- Muse duties: demand signals for every customer message (product, budget, region, district, intent, timing, source kind), customer schedules ("nitanunua wiki ijayo"), five recommendations with a description and why, voice notes shopped from Moran's transcript, products listed by chat from a registered business (text or photo, Muse or the plain reader), "RIPOTI" and dashboard insights (most asked, where, budgets, best sellers, unmet demand) with Muse's reading, best-fit campaign audiences scored from what customers asked for and explained by Muse, Muse-drafted follow-ups. Docs: `docs/muse-duties.md`.
- Order handoff into Moran: a confirmed order for a Moran-linked business is also written into that business's own Moran orders (`MB-` prefixed reference, verified prices, delivery line), its Moran id kept in `orders.external_ref`, and the merchant's accept, reject and delivered decisions are mirrored there; standalone businesses keep the WhatsApp alert only.
- Paying for a service: monthly service prices set in Super Admin → MoranBuy → Billing (audited, blank keeps a service off sale), `POST /api/merchant/services/:service/pay` starts a Snippe or sandbox payment, and only the verified settlement activates the entitlement for the paid period; the follow-up acceptance link now opens a registration page (verification, policy, payment) in the business app.
- Campaign delivery receipts: Moran forwards sent, delivered, read and failed receipts for messages MoranBuy sent to the signed `POST /internal/inbound/status`; campaign recipients and counts and order-alert records follow them, forward only.
- Terms gate: the terms and privacy question with Accept and Reject buttons before anyone is answered, repeated until accepted, versioned and editable in Super Admin; bilingual terms and privacy pages at /terms and /privacy; customers told when the merchant marks an order delivered.
- Scheduled pull of opted-in Moran businesses and products (auto-created merchants, duplicate-free, archiving on a full pass, manual pull from admin); merchants answer order alerts by WhatsApp reply (accept with delivery fee, reject, delivered) or from the dashboard; customers are told the merchant's decision.
- Owner alert on confirmed orders: WhatsApp text or approved template from the MoranBuy number, wording and template set in Super Admin → MoranBuy → Notifications, every outcome recorded in `merchant_notifications`.
- Super Admin integration: a MoranBuy page in weare.moranai.co.tz (register, connect to WhatsApp with Embedded Signup, choose the name, open the dashboard), a signed admin proxy on the Moran backend that carries the admin's identity, MoranBuy accepts it in place of its own login, agent name greeting, weare CSP for Facebook.
- Go-live: admin-entered credentials for Muse Spark, Snippe and Hermes (encrypted, masked, audited, used at runtime without restart), production Compose stack, Nginx site and `.env` example for buy.moranai.co.tz, VPS guide.
- Phase 10 to 15: admin API and app, Moran-side integration patch and drop-in files, Hermes research agent config, skill and client with unverified web offers, security tests, demo seed, architecture, API, deployment, testing and demo docs.
- Phase 7 to 9: campaign engine with templates, Muse drafting with grounding check, audience resolution with exclusion reasons, pricing with configured rates, sandbox and Snippe payment providers with server-side settlement, dispatch gates, explicit confirmation, recipient ledger and worker; new-product outreach offers; optional paid follow-up with offer, leads, schedules, stop rules and worker; consent events, STOP handling and contact erasure; business app tabs for campaigns and follow-up.
- Phase 5 and 6: merchant registration and sign-in, Moran AI account linking with two-factor relay, product and location import keyed by source ids, source-of-truth rules for Moran-owned fields, locations with geocoding or pins, orders and enquiries for merchants, entitlement records enforced in middleware, business app.
